### Tier 4: Real-World Application Scenarios (6 Test Scenarios)

1. `test_t4_scenario_01_early_morning_birth_cutoff_journey`
   - **User Persona & Context**: Somchai, born 05:30 AM on 1992-05-15 in Bangkok. Pre-sunrise (before 06:00 AM cutoff). Selects Tarot cards `[0, 12, 25, 31, 44, 50, 61, 72, 5, 18]`.
   - **Full Session Workflow**:
     1. Submit POST `/api/divine` with `birth_time`: `"05:30"`.
     2. Backend applies 6am cutoff rule -> lunar day shifts to Wednesday. `cutoff_applied: true`.
     3. Backend executes Thai Astrology, Numerology 7x9, Mahabote, and Tarot engines.
     4. Recommender generates 2-digit, 3-digit, and 6-digit lucky numbers.
     5. Backtesting engine matches lucky numbers against 24 GLO draw records in `lottery_results_past_1_year.json`, calculating win counts and heat levels (HOT/WARM/COLD).
     6. Recommender attaches `number_origins` provenance strings for each number.
     7. Oracle Synthesis returns holistic Thai text reading and legal disclaimer.
   - **Expected Assertions**:
     - `status == "success"`
     - `chart.lunar_calendar.cutoff_applied == True`
     - `chart.lunar_calendar.day_of_week == "Wednesday"`
     - `heat_index` contains win count and heat level for every recommended number
     - `number_origins` contains origin array for every recommended number
     - `synthesis` contains non-empty Thai reading text
     - `disclaimer` contains non-empty disclaimer text

2. `test_t4_scenario_02_post_cutoff_morning_birth_journey`
   - **User Persona & Context**: Malai, born 06:30 AM on 1992-05-15 in Chiang Mai. Post-sunrise (after 06:00 AM cutoff). Selects Tarot cards `[1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45]`.
   - **Full Session Workflow**:
     1. Submit POST `/api/divine` with `birth_time`: `"06:30"`.
     2. Backend processes 06:30 AM -> lunar day remains Thursday. `cutoff_applied: false`.
     3. Complete engine execution and backtesting.
   - **Expected Assertions**:
     - `chart.lunar_calendar.cutoff_applied == False`
     - `chart.lunar_calendar.day_of_week == "Thursday"`
     - All R1-R4 sections present in response payload.

3. `test_t4_scenario_03_songkran_new_year_boundary_journey`
   - **User Persona & Context**: Kanya, born on Songkran transition (April 15 23:45 PM vs April 16 06:15 AM).
   - **Full Session Workflow**:
     1. Submit POST `/api/divine` for April 15 23:45 PM.
     2. Submit POST `/api/divine` for April 16 06:15 AM.
   - **Expected Assertions**:
     - Chula Sakarat year in Mahabote shifts accurately between April 15 (Old Year) and April 16 (New Year).
     - Both responses succeed with valid `heat_index` and `number_origins`.

4. `test_t4_scenario_04_midnight_birth_boundary_cards_journey`
   - **User Persona & Context**: Anan, born 00:05 AM (shortly after calendar midnight). Selects edge Tarot card indices `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 73, 74, 75, 76, 77]`.
   - **Full Session Workflow**:
     1. Submit POST `/api/divine` with `birth_time`: `"00:05"` and edge Tarot cards including 0 and 77.

   - **Expected Assertions**:
     - `cutoff_applied == True`
     - Edge Tarot cards (0 = The Fool, 77 = King of Pentacles) processed accurately without index out-of-bounds error.

5. `test_t4_scenario_05_invalid_input_resilience_journey`
   - **User Persona & Context**: User with client-side UI error submitting invalid Tarot selection (`[0..8]`, 9 cards) or malformed birth time (`"invalid-time"`).
